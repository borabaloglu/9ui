## Examples

### Single Date

To select one date, set `mode="single"`.

<ComponentPreview name="calendar-single-demo" />

---

### Multiple Dates

To select several dates, set `mode="multiple"`.

<ComponentPreview name="calendar-multiple-demo" />

---

### Date Range

To select a range, set `mode="range"`. To show two months, set `numberOfMonths={2}`.

<ComponentPreview name="calendar-range-demo" />

---

### Month and Year Selector

To pick the month and year from dropdowns, set `captionLayout="dropdown"`.

<ComponentPreview name="calendar-month-and-year-selector-demo" />

---

### Custom Month and Year Selector

To replace the dropdowns, pass your own `Dropdown` to `components`. This example uses `Select`.

<ComponentPreview name="calendar-custom-month-and-year-selector-demo" />

---

### Week Numbers

To show week numbers, set `showWeekNumber`.

<ComponentPreview name="calendar-week-numbers-demo" />

---

### Disabled

To disable dates, pass them to `disabled`.

<ComponentPreview name="calendar-disabled-demo" />

---

### Presets

To jump to a date from outside the calendar, control `selected` and `month`.

<ComponentPreview name="calendar-presets-demo" />

---

### Custom Cell

To add content to a day, wrap `CalendarDayButton` and pass it as `DayButton` to `components`. To change the cell size, set `--cell-size`.

<ComponentPreview name="calendar-custom-cell-demo" />
