## Tips

<Tips>

<Tip title="Label the textarea">

Give every textarea a `<label>`. A placeholder isn't a label.

```tsx {1}
<Label htmlFor="message">Message</Label>
<Textarea id="message" placeholder="Write your message" />
```

</Tip>

<Tip title="Name a textarea with no visible label">

Set `aria-label` on `Textarea` when there is no visible label.

```tsx {1}
<Textarea aria-label="Message" placeholder="Message" />
```

</Tip>

</Tips>
