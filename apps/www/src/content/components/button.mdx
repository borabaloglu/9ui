## Tips

<Tips>

<Tip title="Style a link as a button">

A button isn't a link. For navigation, put `buttonVariants()` on an `<a>`.

```tsx {1}
<a href="/pricing" className={buttonVariants({ variant: "outline" })}>
	View pricing
</a>
```

</Tip>

<Tip title="Turn off the press scale">

To stop a button from scaling on press, add `[--button-press-scale:1]`.

```tsx
<Button className="[--button-press-scale:1]">Save</Button>
```

</Tip>

</Tips>
