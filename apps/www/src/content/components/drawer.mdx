## About

Drawer is built on the Base UI drawer. The `vaul-base` drawer that used to be installed as `drawer` now lives at [Vaul](/docs/components/vaul), with the same parts under `Vaul*` names.

## Usage

```tsx title="Imports"
import {
	Drawer,
	DrawerClose,
	DrawerDescription,
	DrawerFooter,
	DrawerHeader,
	DrawerPanel,
	DrawerPopup,
	DrawerTitle,
	DrawerTrigger,
} from "@/components/ui/drawer"
```

```tsx title="Anatomy"
<Drawer>
	<DrawerTrigger />
	<DrawerPopup>
		<DrawerHeader>
			<DrawerTitle />
			<DrawerDescription />
		</DrawerHeader>
		<DrawerPanel />
		<DrawerFooter>
			<DrawerClose />
		</DrawerFooter>
	</DrawerPopup>
</Drawer>
```

Put the body in `DrawerPanel`. It scrolls when the content is taller than the drawer, while the header and footer stay in place. With a mouse, text inside the panel can be selected, and the drawer is dragged by its header, footer or handle.

On top and bottom drawers the header, panel and footer keep their content in a centered column, so it stays readable on wide screens.

The drawer pads its edges by `env(safe-area-inset-top)` and `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, so content clears the status bar and home indicator when the page uses `viewport-fit=cover`.

If you do not need swipe gestures or snap points, use [Sheet](/docs/components/sheet).

To change the corner radius, set `className="[--drawer-radius:var(--radius-xl)]"` on `DrawerPopup`.

## Examples

### Swipe Direction

Set `swipeDirection` on `Drawer` to choose the direction that dismisses the drawer. The drawer sits on that edge: `down` is a bottom drawer, `up` a top drawer, `left` and `right` are side drawers.

<ComponentPreview name="drawer-swipe-direction-demo" />

### Inset

Set `variant="inset"` on `DrawerPopup` to float the drawer inside the viewport with rounded corners on every side. Inset drawers are not meant to be combined with snap points.

<ComponentPreview name="drawer-inset-demo" />

### Scrollable Content

`DrawerPanel` scrolls on its own. Swiping down closes the drawer once the panel is scrolled to the top.

<ComponentPreview name="drawer-scrollable-content-demo" />

### Snap Points

Pass `snapPoints` to let a bottom drawer rest at fixed heights. Use pixel or rem strings, or a fraction of the viewport height, and control the current one with `snapPoint` and `onSnapPointChange`. The backdrop stays half visible at the lower snap points and fades in fully as the drawer expands.

<ComponentPreview name="drawer-snap-points-demo" />

### Nested

Render a `Drawer` inside another to stack them. The parent scales down behind the nested one and follows it while it is swiped away.

<ComponentPreview name="drawer-nested-demo" />

### Bare Footer

Set `variant="bare"` on `DrawerFooter` to drop its border and background.

<ComponentPreview name="drawer-footer-bare-demo" />

### Non-modal

Set `modal={false}` on `Drawer` to leave the page behind usable: there is no backdrop, focus is not trapped and the page still scrolls. Add `disablePointerDismissal` to keep the drawer open when something outside is pressed.

<ComponentPreview name="drawer-non-modal-demo" />

### Swipe to Open

Place `DrawerSwipeArea` inside `Drawer` to open the drawer by swiping in from its edge. The area is an invisible strip along that edge of the screen.

The example below runs in a sandbox: the drawer is rendered into the frame with `container`, and the strip is tinted so you can see it. In your app the strip sits on the edge of the screen.

<ComponentPreview name="drawer-swipe-area-demo" />

### Detached Triggers

Create a handle with `createDrawerHandle` and pass it to `Drawer` and to any `DrawerTrigger` outside it. Give each trigger a `payload` and read it from the function passed as the child of `Drawer`.

<ComponentPreview name="drawer-detached-triggers-demo" />

### Handle

The handle shows on top and bottom drawers. Set `showHandle={false}` on `DrawerPopup` to remove it.

### Scale Background

Wrap your app in `DrawerProvider` and put `DrawerIndentBackground` and `DrawerIndent` at the top of the tree to scale the page down while a drawer is open. Every `Drawer` inside the provider triggers it, and the page follows the drawer as it is swiped away.

```tsx
<DrawerProvider>
	<DrawerIndentBackground />
	<DrawerIndent>{children}</DrawerIndent>
</DrawerProvider>
```

`DrawerIndent` is the page: it carries the page background and clips its content to the rounded corners. While a drawer is open, `position: fixed` elements inside it are positioned against it instead of the viewport. With reduced motion the page stays in place.

The example below runs in a sandbox: the drawer is rendered into the frame with `container`, and the frame holds the indent. In your app the indent is the page itself.
