## Tips

<Tips>

<Tip title="Set the height">

Give `ScrollArea` a height or a max height.

```tsx {1}
<ScrollArea className="h-80 w-40 rounded-lg border">
	<div className="p-3 text-sm">
		<p>v1.0.0</p>
		<p>v2.0.0</p>
	</div>
</ScrollArea>
```

</Tip>
